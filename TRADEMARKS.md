# Weft trademarks

Weft™, the Weft logo and weft.zip are trademarks of Alex Miller (AIMHUGE). The code is
licensed under [FSL-1.1-ALv2 with Weft Additional Terms](LICENSE); the name and logo are not. The license says so too
(its Trademarks clause), and the Apache-2.0 license each release turns into after two years
doesn't grant them either (its section 6).

## What's fine without asking

- Saying what your project is built on or works with: "a fork of Weft", "built on Weft",
  "works with Weft".
- Writing about Weft, reviewing it, teaching it, linking to it.
- Running an unmodified build and calling it Weft.

## What needs a different name

- A fork you distribute or host for other people. Give it its own name and icon, and don't use
  names that could be mistaken for it ("Weft Pro", "OpenWeft", "Weft for Teams").
- A product, service, domain, app store listing or social account that uses the name or logo
  in a way that suggests it's made or endorsed by Weft.
